# A five-minute first practice

1. Start the app using the README. Open Local AI setup; check the connection.
2. Try a sample to explore synthetic demo mode. Its feedback is fixed, not live AI.
3. For real practice, start over and switch to live AI. Paste your CV and a job description. Remove contact details and anything you would rather not save.
4. Choose gentle difficulty and a focus. Leave Save this session locally unchecked for temporary practice, or select it to keep your work on this machine.
5. Answer one question in your own words. Read what went well, corrections, clarity advice, and the example answer. Verify advice; flag questionable feedback. Hypothetical examples are not your personal experience.
6. Retry an answer or move on. Finish to see strengths, revision topics and the next practice suggestion.
7. Open Past sessions to revisit saved practice. Delete an individual session or all local data when you want.

No score predicts hiring success. Clearer explanations take repetition. Use your actual projects and experiences, particularly for STAR answers.
